Online Safety Basics for Everyday Internet Use

This article includes my own experience and is part of the MR RUKA’s HUB content journey.

Online safety involves protecting accounts, devices and personal information through simple habits.

Use unique passwords

Do not reuse an important password across multiple accounts. A reputable password manager can help.

Turn on two-step verification

Where available, enable two-step verification on email, social media and other important accounts.

Check links before signing in

If an unexpected message asks you to sign in, open the official website or app yourself instead of using a suspicious link.

Keep software updated

Use official updates for your operating system, browser and apps.

Protect personal information

Think carefully before publicly posting phone numbers, addresses, identity documents or other sensitive information.

What I Keep in Mind as a New Creator

Building an online presence has also made me think more carefully about how I use digital tools and share information online. As I develop MR RUKA’s HUB, I want to learn continuously and build the platform responsibly.
