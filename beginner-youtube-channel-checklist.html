Beginner’s Guide to Starting a YouTube Channel

Draft for MR RUKA’s HUB. Add your own examples, screenshots, testing or experience before publishing as final editorial content.

Starting a YouTube channel can feel complicated because there are many things to think about at once. A simple checklist makes the process easier.

1. Choose a clear topic

Choose something you enjoy making and can continue creating about, such as gaming, technology, tutorials, reviews or entertainment.

2. Create simple branding

Choose a recognisable channel name, profile image and visual style. Consistency matters more than complicated graphics.

3. Plan before recording

Write down the purpose of each video, the main points and the action you want to demonstrate.

4. Use accurate titles and thumbnails

Your title should describe the video honestly. A thumbnail should be easy to understand and should not promise something the video does not contain.

5. Review your own videos

Watch your uploads as a viewer and note unclear explanations, unnecessary pauses and improvements for the next video.
