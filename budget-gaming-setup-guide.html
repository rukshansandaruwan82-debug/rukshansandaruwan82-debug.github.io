Budget Gaming Setup Guide for Beginners

This article includes my own experience and is part of the MR RUKA’s HUB content journey.

A good gaming setup does not have to start with expensive equipment. For a beginner, comfort, stable performance and reliable audio are often more important than decorative accessories.

Start with what you have

Understand the games you want to play and the limits of your current device before buying upgrades.

Prioritise comfort

A comfortable chair, sensible screen height and enough desk space can make longer sessions easier. Take regular breaks.

Think about audio

Clear headphones can improve game audio and communication. For creators, a basic microphone can also be a useful upgrade.

Organise cables

Simple cable management keeps the setup cleaner and easier to maintain.

Upgrade one thing at a time

Identify the biggest problem first, then buy the item that actually solves it.
