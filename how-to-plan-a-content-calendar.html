How to Plan a Simple Monthly Content Calendar

Draft for MR RUKA’s HUB. Add your own examples, screenshots, testing or experience before publishing as final editorial content.

A content calendar can be as simple as a list of topics and planned publishing dates. It helps creators stay organised without needing to invent an idea every day.

Choose a realistic schedule

Start with a schedule you can maintain. One well-prepared upload can be better than several rushed uploads.

Use content categories

MR RUKA’s HUB can use categories such as Gaming, Technology, Creator Tips and Entertainment.

Plan different formats

Mix videos, short tips, articles and updates while keeping the overall brand consistent.

Leave room for new ideas

Do not fill every day. Keep space for topics that come from your own projects and experiences.

Review the month

Write down which topics you enjoyed making and which ones viewers responded to, then use those observations for the next month.
