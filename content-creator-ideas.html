10 Content Ideas for a New Creator

This article includes my own experience and is part of the MR RUKA’s HUB content journey.

One of the hardest parts of being a new creator is deciding what to publish next. Repeatable formats make planning easier.

  1. A beginner guide to something you know well.
  2. A step-by-step tutorial with screenshots.
  3. A review based on your actual experience.
  4. A comparison that explains genuine differences.
  5. A list of common mistakes and solutions.
  6. A behind-the-scenes look at your workflow.
  7. A troubleshooting guide for a problem you solved.
  8. A budget setup guide.
  9. A monthly channel update.
  10. A “what I learned” article after a project.

Choose ideas you can support

The strongest topics are those where you can add your own examples, testing, screenshots or experience.

Why I Want to Create Different Content

My plans for MR RUKA’s HUB are not limited to one type of content. I want to create many kinds of content instead of stopping with just one subject.

I want to keep experimenting, learning and finding useful or interesting ideas that I can share. My goal is to keep developing the HUB over time and build a platform that people can return to for different types of content.
