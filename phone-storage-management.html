How to Free Up Phone Storage Safely

This article includes my own experience and is part of the MR RUKA’s HUB content journey.

Running out of phone storage can be frustrating. The safest approach is to review large files before deleting anything.

Find what uses the most space

Open your phone’s storage settings and identify the largest categories such as videos, photos, apps and downloads.

Review large videos

Keep important originals and delete only files you have confirmed are backed up or no longer needed.

Check Downloads

Review old installers, documents and duplicate files individually before deleting them.

Remove unused apps

Uninstall apps you no longer use, but avoid deleting system components unless you know what they do.

Back up first

If you use cloud backup, verify that important files have actually uploaded before removing local copies.

Why I Care About Keeping My Digital Work Organised

As I work on content, I deal with files, ideas and media regularly. Keeping digital files organised is useful because content creation can quickly create a lot of photos, videos and project files.

I am still developing my own workflow, but I have learned that simple organisation can make it easier to find what I need when I am ready to work on the next piece of content.
