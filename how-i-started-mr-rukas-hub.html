MR RUKA’s HUB started with a simple idea: create a place where entertainment, technology and useful digital content can come together. A YouTube channel can become a community where viewers discover ideas, learn something useful and return for new content.

Why build a website alongside YouTube?

A website gives a creator another place to organise information. A video can demonstrate a process, while an article can provide written steps, extra details and screenshots.

What I want to publish

  • Gaming and gaming-related content
  • Technology tips and tutorials
  • Creator tips and lessons from my own experience
  • Entertainment and channel updates

The goal of the HUB

The long-term goal is to keep improving content quality and make the website a useful home for the MR RUKA’s HUB community.
