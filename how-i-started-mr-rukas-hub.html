MY EXPERIENCE

Why I Started MR RUKA’s HUB

MR RUKA’s HUB started from a simple idea: I wanted to share what I know with other people, keep learning, and build a place where different types of useful content can come together.

Starting again

I have tried creating YouTube content before, but it did not go the way I wanted. I learned that having an idea is only the beginning. Creating consistently, learning how to manage a channel and improving each video are just as important.

Now I am starting again from the beginning, with a clearer plan and a stronger focus on learning. I believe I can improve by creating, reviewing my work and continuing to learn from every upload.

What I am learning

Content creation has helped me explore video editing, YouTube channel management, creative planning and new digital tools. I use tools such as CapCut for editing and AI tools to support parts of my workflow, while keeping my own ideas and decisions at the centre of the content.

What I want this platform to become

I do not want MR RUKA’s HUB to be limited to only one type of content. I plan to explore technology, gaming, creator tips, useful digital guides, entertainment and other projects that I believe are worth sharing.

My bigger goal is to build something useful for people who visit the website or watch the channel. If the platform eventually earns money, I also hope that success can help me support people who need help.
