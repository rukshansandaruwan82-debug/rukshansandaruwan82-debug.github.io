A Simple Video Editing Workflow for Beginners

Draft for MR RUKA’s HUB. Add your own examples, screenshots, testing or experience before publishing as final editorial content.

Video editing becomes easier when you follow the same basic process each time.

Step 1: Organise files

Keep original clips, licensed music, images and project files in clearly named folders.

Step 2: Build the main sequence

Put useful clips in order before adding effects. First make the story or explanation clear.

Step 3: Remove unnecessary parts

Cut mistakes, long pauses and repeated information while keeping a natural pace.

Step 4: Improve audio

Make speech understandable and keep background music at a sensible level. Use media you have permission to use.

Step 5: Add useful graphics

Titles, labels and screenshots can help viewers follow the video when they add information.

Step 6: Watch the final export

Check the complete exported file before publishing, including audio and on-screen text.
